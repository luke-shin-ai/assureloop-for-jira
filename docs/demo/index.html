AssureLoop for Jira

Evidence, not spreadsheets.

This is a static demo. No install, no Atlassian site, no server, and nothing here talks to anything. It renders the twenty claims the app ships with, in the states measured on the demo site on 2026-11-20 (18 verified, 1 stale, 1 missing). Pressing Why? shows the read that produced the verdict. The app itself is where a scan runs against your own work items.

What this guarantees: fixed rules over the Teamwork Graph and REST, the evidence object and its observation time, and a named blind spot when the app could not look.

      Close the gaps

      One Jira issue per missing or stale claim, assigned through the owner route the app resolved (graph page owner, then issue assignee, then component lead, then control owner), with that route written into the issue description. Re-scanning does not open a second issue for the same claim: each one carries a finding token in its summary.

      On this programme, AC-02 raised PAY-14 and AC-06 raised PAY-13. The other eighteen claims are verified and raise nothing.

      Audit pack

      A workpaper, not a dashboard: one row per claim, and a fingerprint a reviewer can recompute.

      The digest shown is a SHA-256 cut to 128 bits, so 32 hex characters. The full 64-hex value is what the app compares.

      Unexplained gaps: 1 of 2

      AC-06's stale claim carries a recorded exception: a reason, an expiry of 2027-01-31, and a compensating control. An exception never changes a verdict. It moves the gap from unexplained to explained and discloses it on the pack with its author.

      AC-02's missing claim has no exception, so it stays unexplained. That number is what a closing meeting asks about first.

      What this does not guarantee: work done outside this app, evidence types with no connector here, and whether a recorded exception is substantively right.
